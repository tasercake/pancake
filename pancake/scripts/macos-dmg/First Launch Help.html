Open Pancake Stacker for the first time

Drag the app to Applications and open it from there. If macOS blocks it, confirm you downloaded it from Pancake’s GitHub Releases before continuing.

  1. Open System Settings and select Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to Security. After a blocked launch, an option to open Pancake Stacker appears here. If you see Open first, click it.
  3. Choose Open Anyway for Pancake Stacker and confirm when prompted.
