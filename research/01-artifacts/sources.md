## Retrieval audit and unresolved evidence

- HTML from arXiv was used for S08–S11. An unversioned S11 PDF exceeded web-tool size limit; `html/2311.17846v1` succeeded. No local full-paper archive was created.
- S14 and S15 have partial publisher access only. Their evidence is limited to retrieved original abstracts/excerpts. Their methods need full-text review before implementing any detector.
- Search also returned forum threads, news pages and broad surveys. These were discovery aids, not used to establish technical claims.
- S07 uses the current version 9 manual. Software comparisons still need exact versions and code commits; author documentation is not an independent quality benchmark.
- There is no prevalence survey here, no measured frequency/severity distribution across photographers, and no independently validated complete artifact detector. The taxonomy is an engineering synthesis.
- Material correctness, a finite-aperture target appearance and strict reference-view visibility remain design decisions. A visually successful clone/retouch is not proof of recovered scene content.
