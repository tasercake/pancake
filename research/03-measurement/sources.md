# Measurement sources and evidence limits

Access date: **2026-11-15**. Primary papers, author pages/code and official standards were used. The registry's artifact-specific formulas, counts, eligibility rules and qualification gates are **engineering proposals**, not claims that these exact detectors were published or validated. Citations identify prior art or motivation unless a standard metric is explicitly named. No numerical macro ranking is imported from these sources.

## Retrieval and reproducibility notes

MFIFB pinned revision: `a3cc5104613c97c58e9c9f9da9fd075bd72c12f8`. FLIP pinned revision: `d3ab02a4425eb167d8a4021b382a2bafc6e6c838`. Their READMEs were inspected through GitHub connector; no binaries, weights, archive metric code or test images downloaded. Pins identify what was read, not an executed dependency lockfile.

Search topics included source-conditioned fusion metrics, human/objective agreement, differentiable-metric attacks, texture tolerance, camera SFR, lens/pupil image formation, subjective/recognition standards and uncertainty. Search results from surveys, news and automated summaries were discovery aids only. Primary abstract-only access is labeled above.

The statistical formulas and cohort policies are proposed study design. Sensitivity/false-alarm goals, scene counts, metric thresholds and viewing choices require pilot calibration and preregistration. Nothing here establishes a validated global stacking score.
